# Awesome-Spend-Management-Platform

## Top Spend Management Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Corporate Cards, Expense Management, Procure-to-Pay, AP Automation, Budgets & Spend Control*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Spend Management**. These tools help finance teams control and automate company spending through corporate cards, expense reports, procurement workflows, bill pay, approvals, and real-time visibility.



**Examples** include Ramp, Brex, Airbase, Spendesk, Emburse, Pleo, Moss, Divvy (BILL Spend & Expense), Teampay, Mesh Payments, Coupa, Zip, Precoro, Procurify, Payhawk, Medius, Ivalua, GEP SMART, Rippling Spend, and Rho (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured modern spend platforms with corporate cards and real-time controls are almost entirely commercial. Open-source alternatives center on **ERPNext**, **Odoo Community**, and related ERP/expense modules that provide procurement, expenses, approvals, and accounting. Dedicated open-source corporate-card style platforms are rare. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Fully open-source ERP with strong procurement, purchasing, expense claims, approval workflows, accounting, and inventory modules. One of the most complete open-source foundations for spend-related processes.



- **[Odoo Community](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Popular open-source business suite including expense management, purchase, accounting, and approval apps that can be combined into a spend control stack.



- **[ERPNext Expenses modules & extensions](https://github.com/kid1194/erpnext_expenses)**  

  Community modules and customizations that extend ERPNext with additional expense management capabilities.



- **[metasfresh](https://github.com/metasfresh/metasfresh)**  

  Open-source ERP system with purchasing, accounting, and related financial modules suitable for organizations seeking a full business suite.



- **[Dolibarr and other open-source ERPs](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  Lighter open-source ERP/CRM systems that include expense and purchasing features for smaller organizations.



- **[Expense & finance self-hosted tools](https://github.com/search?q=expense+management+OR+spend+OR+procurement+open+source)**  

  Community projects for expense tracking, reimbursement workflows, and basic procurement that can be self-hosted.



- **[Personal / small-team finance managers](https://github.com/search?q=self-hosted+expense+OR+finance+manager)**  

  Privacy-focused open-source finance tools that some small teams adapt for simple spend tracking (generally not full corporate spend platforms).



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Approval workflow engines**: Open-source BPM and workflow tools used to build multi-level spend approvals.

- **Accounting integrations**: Connectors and modules linking open-source ERPs to banks and payment providers.

- **Document & invoice OCR**: Open-source or self-hosted tools for capturing receipts and invoices.

- **Budgeting & reporting**: Analytics stacks built on ERP data for spend visibility.

- **Procurement request portals**: Lightweight open-source request and catalog systems.

- Policy-as-code experiments for enforcing spend rules in custom stacks.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

For the closest open-source path to spend management, start with **ERPNext** or **Odoo Community** and configure purchasing, expense claims, multi-level approvals, and accounting.  

These provide solid procure-to-pay and expense foundations without licensing costs.  

Modern corporate-card issuing, real-time card controls, automated receipt matching, and AI policy enforcement remain the domain of commercial platforms (Ramp, Brex, Spendesk, Coupa, Airbase, etc.).  

Many organizations use open-source ERP for core accounting and procurement while adopting a commercial spend/card platform for employee-facing controls and automation.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Spend management systems handle sensitive financial data and payment credentials. Strong security, access controls, audit trails, and compliance with applicable financial regulations are essential.

- Open-source ERP and expense tools offer transparency and cost control but require implementation effort, ongoing maintenance, and careful integration with banking/payment providers. They do not replace regulated card-issuing or payment services offered by commercial platforms.
